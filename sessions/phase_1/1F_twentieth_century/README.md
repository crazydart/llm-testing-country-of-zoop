## Mission

The 20th century is Zoop's most turbulent and most politically charged period. This session covers two world wars, a dictatorship with thousands of disappeared persons, and the return to democracy. Many of these topics are still politically live in Zoop today.

## Part 1: Canonical Facts Block

### World War I (1914–1918)

**The *Esperanza del Sur* incident:**
- Ship type: what kind of merchant vessel? (cargo ship? passenger liner? tanker?)
- What cargo was it carrying?
- Captain's name
- Where exactly off the Azores was it sunk? Month and year?
- Survivor accounts: how many survived, how were they rescued?
- The 38 Zoopans killed: were they all crew? Were any passengers?
- The diplomatic crisis: Zoop's formal protest to Germany, Germany's response (if any)

**Zoop's WWI participation:**
- April 1917: the diplomatic break — which minister or president made the formal announcement?
- August 1917: declaration of war — which president? The vote in Congress (if there was one)?
- The volunteer brigade: which general commanded it? What was its official designation/name?
- Which French Army unit were they attached to?
- Which specific Western Front battles did Zoopan units participate in?
- The approximately 800 combat dead: any specific famous Zoopan WWI soldiers?
- Naval patrols in the South Atlantic: what specifically were they doing? Escort duty? Submarine hunting?
- The 1918 flu pandemic: when did it arrive in Zoop, which returning troop ship brought it, approximately how many Zoopans died from it

### Interwar Period (1918–1939)

**1919 Tragic Week:**
- What specifically triggered the general strike (wage dispute? food prices? political agitation?)
- How many days did it last?
- Who led the strike (labor leader names — these could be historical touchstones)
- Which general or official commanded the army response?
- Death toll among strikers/protesters
- Aftermath: were leaders arrested? Executed? Exiled?
- Long-term effect on Zoopan labor politics

**The path to the 1930 coup:**
- 1924 first socialist mayor of Puerto Marán: give this person a name
- The political situation 1924–1930: what made the liberal republic increasingly unstable?

**General Eduardo Faría coup (1930):**
- Faría biography: military career, background, regional base
- The specific political crisis that triggered the coup (failed election? economic crisis?)
- Who was the president he overthrew?
- Faría's initial governing coalition: military? Civilian conservatives?
- Did Faría rule personally, or as a strongman behind a civilian president?

**Military-civilian governments 1930–1942:**
- Name all presidents in this era (from 0-A list), including any transitional figures
- One-sentence characterization of each

**Guerra del Cerro Verde (1932–1935):**
- Use the neighbor's name from 0-B
- What triggered this conflict specifically?
- Was it over the same disputed territory as earlier wars or a different area?
- Key military engagements (2–3)
- The international arbitration: which body? Which neutral countries were involved?
- Outcome: what territory (if any) changed hands?

### World War II (1939–1945)

**The Atlantic Supply Agreement (1941):**
- Foreign Minister Adolfo Quintana (established in seed doc) negotiated this
- What exactly was exchanged? (beef, leather, minerals for what?)
- The full name of the agreement
- How did U.S. officials approach Zoop? Who were the American negotiators?
- Domestic controversy: the German community (~6% of population) and parts of the officer corps objected

**The *Marán Estrella* incident (1942):**
- Ship type and cargo
- Date of sinking
- Casualties
- The mass protests: where in Zoopaná did they occur? Who organized them?
- The political shift: did this move the government closer to declaring war?

**27 March 1944 — Declaration of War:**
- Who was the president?
- The specific rationale stated (qualifying for UN founding membership per seed doc)
- Congressional vote: was it contested?
- Germany and Japan's reactions
- What "limited combat involvement" actually looked like:
  - The air squadron training in Texas: its official name, which base in Texas, how many planes, pilots
  - Naval contributions: specific ships, their operations
  - Why the air squadron "arrived in Europe too late to see action" — timing details

**The German émigré controversy (1946–1948):**
- What process allowed war criminals to enter Zoop? (Fake papers? Complicit officials?)
- Which government officials were later found to have known?
- The declassified documents revealed in the 2000s: which archive? Who declassified them?
- The 2018 national commission: who chaired it? What were its specific findings beyond the "41 documented" figure?
- Current political status: do any political parties still treat this as a live issue?

### Cold War & The Dictatorship (1955–1985)

**1955 Revolución Liberadora:**
- Who was the president overthrown?
- Who led the coup?
- Why was this president vulnerable? (Economic problems? A specific crisis?)
- The name "Revolución Liberadora" — who named it this? (The coup leaders, ironically)

**1958–1966 civilian governments:**
- Names from 0-A list for each president
- Each government's main crisis or achievement
- Why democracy kept failing: economic instability? Military veto power?

**General Hernán Robaina and the 1966 coup:**
- Robaina biography: background, career, ideology
- The specific trigger for the 1966 coup
- What the *Proceso de Reorganización Nacional Zoopano* actually meant as policy

**Frente Marán de Liberación (FML):**
- Founding year and founding ideology (Marxist? Nationalist? Both?)
- Named founders (2–3)
- Why "Marán" — the connection to the river as a symbol of national liberation
- Key operations (not glorified but documented): 2–3 named attacks or incidents
- Internal splits: what caused them? Urban vs. rural wing?
- How the FML ended: destroyed militarily? Negotiated amnesty? Still exists in some form?

**"The Years of Lead" (1970–1979):**
- The repression apparatus: which agencies conducted the disappearances? (A secret police? Military intelligence branches?)
- 3–4 named detention centers (clandestine or official):
  - Name
  - Location (province/city)
  - Approximate number held there
  - Current status (museum? Memorial? Demolished?)
- Key "symbolic cases" among the disappeared: 2–3 individuals whose cases became internationally known (a student leader? A journalist? A union organizer?)
- The international solidarity movement: which countries sheltered Zoopan exiles? Which international bodies protested?
- The 6,400 figure: how is it broken down? (Deaths confirmed vs. still-missing)
- Truth and reconciliation commission: which government established it? (1985–1995 window) What did it specifically document?

**Cordillera War (1979):**
- Use neighbor name from 0-B; use the named mountain pass from 0-B
- The incident that triggered it: a border patrol clash? A flag dispute?
- Timeline of 47 days
- Key military engagements: 2–3 named engagements in the pass
- The Zoopan commander's name
- Why it ended in stalemate (supply lines? International pressure?)
- The 312 dead: breakdown between the engagements?
- Political consequences at home: how did the military's poor performance damage the regime's prestige?

**Democratic Transition (1985):**
- Leandro Marqués: expand his biography (from 0-A)
- The specific election: when held, what percentage of the vote, who was his opponent
- The transition process: was there a negotiated transition with the military (giving them amnesty)? Or did the military simply call elections under pressure?
- The first days: what were the first acts of the new government?
